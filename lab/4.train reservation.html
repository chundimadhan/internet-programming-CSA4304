Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a train ticket?

Login to your account, search for a train, select the class and seat, enter passenger details and complete the payment.

What is PNR?

PNR is the unique Passenger Name Record number generated after a successful booking.

How can I check my PNR status?

Enter your PNR number in the PNR Status section to view the booking status.

Can I cancel my ticket?

Yes. Enter your PNR number and submit a cancellation request.
